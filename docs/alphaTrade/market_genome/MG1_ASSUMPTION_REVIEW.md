### MG1A.COMMON.LIFECYCLE.001

**Disposition:** `REVISE`; **Freeze gate:** `BLOCKED`

**Question:** Freeze common transitions, terminal reset behavior, and family-specific phase semantics.

**Affected families:** `ALL`

**Parameter refs:** `common_lifecycle`

**Evidence:** `PLAN_MG1A`, `TECH_LIFECYCLE`

Retain the eight-phase causal lifecycle, but do not reset a terminal object to INACTIVE under the same pattern_id. Terminal objects remain immutable; a later formation creates a new object and may link to its predecessor.

**Approved invariants**

- The eight common phase names remain the shared lifecycle vocabulary.
- Online transitions use only evidence available at or before as_of_eob.
- Any pre-terminal formation phase may transition to INVALIDATED.

**Required changes**

- Remove same-object COMPLETED-to-INACTIVE and INVALIDATED-to-INACTIVE resets.
- Define earliest causal evidence for each family phase and persistent-family completion.

**Blocking items**

- OBJECT_ID_RESET_POLICY_NOT_APPLIED
- FAMILY_PHASE_EVIDENCE_NOT_DEFINED
